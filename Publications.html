Publications

Molecular survey of Bartonella species in stray cats and dogs, humans, and questing ticks from Portugal. Torrejón, E., […], Domingos, A., & Antunes, S. Pathogens, 2022. IP IF2022 3.4

This article presents the findings of my master’s thesis, where I used qPCR to identify Bartonella DNA in 20% of stray cats in the Lisbon area. This research highlighted the emergence of Bartonella henselae in cat reservoirs, drawing attention to the rise of this pathogen in Europe.


Proteomic Profiling of Plasma and Gut-Derived Extracellular Vesicles in Obesity. Pereira, P. B., Torrejón, E., […], Macedo M. P. & Machado de Oliveira, R. Nutrients, 2024. IP IF2023 4.8

For this article, I contributed to the development and implementation of a proteomics analysis pipeline that highlights changes in post-translational modifications. This pipeline was used to compare plasma and gut-derived EV proteins across control and prediabetic conditions. Additionally, I mentored a master’s student throughout the final stages of the project and played a key role in preparing the manuscript. This work was completed during my first year of PhD at NOVA Medical School.


Aldehyde Dehydrogenase 2 (ALDH2): a novel sorafenib target in hepatocellular carcinoma unraveled by the proteome-wide cellular thermal shift assay. Ferreira, I. C. + , Torrejón, E.+, […], Bortoluzzi, A. & Rebelo, S. P. SLAS Discovery, 2024. IP IF2023 3.3

+These authors contributed equally.

The discovery of ALDH2 as a new target for Sorafenib (a drug to treat liver cancer) resulted from two years of research, during which we established a CETSA-MS technology platform at iBET. I was responsible for both wet and dry lab tasks, ranging from the preparation and optimization of CETSA and CETSA-MS protocols for data acquisition to implementing a tailored bioinformatics workflow for thermal proteome profiling. Additionally, I contributed to the target engagement validation assays and manuscript preparation.


Adapting beyond borders: insights from the 19th student council symposium (SCS2023), the first hybrid ISCB student council global event. Al Sium, S. M., Torrejón, […] & Olguín-Orellana, G. J. Bioinformatics Advances, 2024. IP IF20243 2.84

This article showcases the achievements of the 19th ISCB Student Council Symposium. As part of the organizing team, I led the Website Committee and coordinated a roundtable discussion on the impact of AI in bioinformatics. Additionally, I co-led the manuscript preparation alongside Al Sium.


Molecular Survey of Anaplasmataceae Agents, Rickettsia spp., Bartonella spp., and Piroplasmids in Ectoparasites from Cave-Dwelling Bats in Mainland Portugal. Sanches, G. S., Rodrigues, L., Torrejón, E., […] & André, M. R. Pathogens, 2025. IP IF2023 3.4

Molecular and phylogenetic insights into Polychromophilus diversity in bat flies (Diptera: Nycteribiidae) from Portugal. André, M. R., de Mello, V. V. C., Barbier, E., Mariano, L. C., Rodrigues, L., Torrejón, E., […] & Sanches, G. S. Parasitology International, 2026. IF2024 1.9

I contributed to these work during my master’s years by participating in field trips to collect ectoparasites and identify bat hosts. I was also involved in the subsequent ectoparasites species identification in the lab.


EV-Net: A computational framework to model extracellular vesicles-mediated communication. Torrejon, E., […] & Machado de Oliveira, R. bioRxiv, 2026. (pre-print)

During my second PhD year, I was awarded an EMBO short scientific exchange grant to develop EV-Net, an R package built during a research stay at the MMG Systems Biomedicine Lab (led by A. Baudot). EV-Net enables exploration of the effects of extracellular vesicles on tissues of interest by ranking vesicle proteomic content using a weighted network derived from protein-protein interaction and regulatory databases. For more information, visit EV-Net’s website: https://torrejonia.github.io/EV-Net/
